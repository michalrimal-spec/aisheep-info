Data Deletion Instructions

aisheep · Privacy Policy

aisheep is a personal, single-user application. It stores only an access token for the Facebook Pages, TikTok accounts and YouTube channels controlled by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It does not collect data about other people.

How to delete or revoke

  1. Facebook: open Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), find AIsheep Publisher and click Remove. The access is revoked immediately and the stored token becomes invalid.
  2. TikTok: open TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services (or Manage app permissions), find the aisheep app and click Remove access. The stored token becomes invalid.
  3. YouTube / Google: revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  4. Stored token: after revocation the owner deletes the token file from the local computer.

To request deletion by e-mail, write to operosus23@gmail.com; the request is handled within 30 days.
