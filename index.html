aisheep

aisheep is a personal, single-user tool that prepares short educational videos with animal facts, records and curiosities and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels (Fur & Fact and Zvěřinec zajímavostí).

What the application does

  • Generates scripts, voice-over, images and videos from verified, sourced facts.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the owner's YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), and can schedule their publication.

Google user data

The application requests only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, solely to upload videos to YouTube channels controlled by the owner of the application. It does not read, collect, store or share any other Google user data. The application is not offered to the public.
