What aisheep is

aisheep is a personal, single-user application operated by its owner. It prepares short educational videos with animal facts and publishes them to social media accounts and pages that the owner controls (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok). It is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not publish content on behalf of third parties.

Use of the application

  • The application is used only by its owner, only with accounts the owner controls.
  • Published videos are created from sourced facts and are labelled as AI-assisted content where the platform provides such a label.
  • The application respects the terms and policies of each platform, including the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Content Sharing Guidelines.
