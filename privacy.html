Privacy Policy

aisheep · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

aisheep is a personal, single-user application operated by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Contact: operosus23@gmail.com.

What data the application accesses

The application uses Google OAuth with the single scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It is used only to upload videos (and set their title, description, tags and visibility or scheduled publication time) to YouTube channels that are controlled by the owner of the application.

The application does not read, collect or analyse any other Google user data, such as your YouTube viewing history, subscriptions, comments, contacts, e-mail or files.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token issued by Google is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file outside of any public repository. It is not transmitted to any third party and is used only to authenticate uploads to the YouTube Data API.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. We do not use Google user data for advertising or for training machine-learning models.

Compliance

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token is then deleted from the owner's computer. For any question write to operosus23@gmail.com.
